03 — Process

A simple, predictable process

Three steps from first message to finished site.

  1. 01

    Discuss

    We talk about your goals, your customers, and what the site needs to do. You get a clear plan and a fixed scope before anything is built.

  2. 02

    Build

    I design and build the site, checking it on real screen sizes as I go. You can follow progress and give feedback along the way.

  3. 03

    Review

    We go through it together, I make the final tweaks, and you get a clean, well-organised site you can keep or grow.
